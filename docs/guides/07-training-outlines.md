# 30-minute training outlines, one page per pilot department

Use with the role guides in this folder. Bring phones or the shop tablet. Each person signs in with their **own** login and changes the password first.

Every tap is sent at once and there is no Undo. Practise on the practice copy Swayam names, not on live DESPL-320, for anything you do not want kept on the record.

---

## 1. Planning/PPC (guide 04)

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Sign in, change password, open **Today**. |
| 5 | Show **Start** and **Done** on one office process (1 to 11). Show **Earlier** (up to about 3 working days) and the late reason. |
| 10 | Show **New job**, **Copy a previous job**, the five steps, the required delivery date, **Save and exit** and **Resume**. Say who taps **Create job**. |
| 5 | Show **Materials**, **Release BOM**, **Weld map**, **Drawings** (read only for Planning). |
| 5 | Questions. How to report a problem. |

**Five-question check**

1. Which processes can you tick on Today? (Office processes 1 to 11 only.)
2. What is required before a job can be scheduled? (The committed delivery date.)
3. Can you create the job from your draft? (No. The Production Head or Admin does.)
4. When do floor steps appear for a new job? (After Engineering releases the BOM.)
5. You tapped Done on the wrong process. What now? (There is no Undo. Tell the Production Head or an admin.)

## 2. Fabrication Prep (guide 01)

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Sign in, change password. **Scan** a traveller QR. Type a serial if the camera fails. |
| 10 | One supervisor and one operator do a real **Start** and **Done** on a cutting or edge-preparation step. Show **Also for SR02 to SR09**. |
| 8 | Cutting a pressure part: show the red card **Can't start yet**. Show **Notify Stores** and **Pick heat number**. Show **Notify Engineering** for a drawing not released. |
| 4 | MTC Verification: the Fabrication Prep supervisor ticks it. Show **Earlier** and the late reason. |
| 3 | **Not sent, Try again**. No Undo. |

**Five-question check**

1. What two things must exist before you cut a pressure part? (Material issued and a heat number picked.)
2. The card says "Can clear it: Stores". What do you tap? (Notify Stores.)
3. Can you type the heat number yourself? (No. You pick it from Stores' list.)
4. Who ticks MTC Verification? (The Fabrication Prep supervisor.)
5. You see **Not sent**. What do you do? (Tap Try again when Wi-Fi is back. Do not tap Done twice.)

## 3. Fabrication/Welding (guide 01)

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Sign in. **Scan**. Read the unit page: Shell, heads, nozzles. |
| 8 | Do **Start** and **Done** on a fit-up, then a weld step. Show the **Welder for LS-1** pick. Show **Also for SR02 to SR09**. |
| 7 | Try to weld before fit-up on the same joint. Read the refusal. Show **Out of usual order** and **Start early** (supervisor, with a reason). |
| 5 | Late reason. **Returned by QC**. |
| 5 | Hard stops: no welding on a pressure part after final heat treatment or hydro, hydro before heat treatment, painting before hydro. Say: call QC. |

**Five-question check**

1. What comes before a weld on the same joint? (Fit-up. NDT comes after.)
2. Who can use **Start early**? (Supervisor and above, with a reason.)
3. What do you pick when you mark a weld done? (The welder.)
4. The step reads "Returned by QC". What now? (Fix it, tap Done again.)
5. Can anyone skip the rule that stops painting before hydro? (No.)

## 4. QC (guide 02)

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Sign in. Open **Queue**. Explain the three tabs and the **Job** filter. |
| 8 | **Accept** one step. **Reject...** one with a reason. Show the NCR it opens and **Returned by QC** on the floor. |
| 7 | **Open holds**: **Record...**, **Clear point**, **Reject point**. Show the extra fields for a third-party point and a hydro point. |
| 7 | Rule NCR: **Scan**, open the unit, **Raise NCR (H5)**. Show **Repair**, the Production Head's **Concession** and your **Ratify**. |
| 3 | Weld map **Confirm N joints**. Maker-checker. |

**Five-question check**

1. Can you accept work you did yourself? (No. A different person must.)
2. What is needed to reject a step? (A reason.)
3. What must you record to clear a hydro hold point? (Test pressure, hold time and gauge IDs.)
4. Who can raise the H5 or H6 NCR? (Only QC, from the unit page.)
5. Who ratifies a concession the Production Head accepted? (A QC user. It closes the NCR.)
