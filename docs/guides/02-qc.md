# QC guide

For QC inspectors. Print on one sheet, both sides.

## Your queue

Open **Queue** in the menu. It has three tabs, oldest first, grouped by job and unit. Use the **Job** filter to narrow it.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Ready to inspect** | Work the floor sent to you, and inspection steps that only QC records. |
| **Open holds** | Hold points and witness points waiting for a result. |
| **NCRs** | Rejection reports that need a decision. |

## Accept or Reject

Each row says what the work is, who did it ("Done by Ravi") and how long it has waited.

- **Accept** closes the step.
- **Reject...** needs a reason ("What is wrong"). Tap **Reject**. A rejection report (NCR) opens by itself. The supervisor sees **Returned by QC** and your reason.
- You **cannot accept work you did yourself**. The message says a different person must do the second step. Ask another QC colleague. Ask the Production Head to give the pilot a second QC login.
- An inspection step is refused if the work before it is not done: "The work this inspection checks is not done yet." Wait for the floor.

## Hold points

Open the **Open holds** tab. Blocking points show first (H, then R&A, RW, W).

1. Tap **Record...**
2. Fill what the point needs. A third-party point needs the **inspector's name** and the **report or IRN number**. A hydro point needs **test pressure, hold time and gauge IDs**. The screen tells you what is missing.
3. Tap **Clear point**, or **Reject point** (remarks are required). A rejected point shows **Reinspect**. Your next attempt is a new line. The old one stays on record.

A stage with an open blocking hold point cannot be completed. A waiver for a witness point is approved by the Production Head ("Waiver pending").

## Welding after heat treatment or hydro (H5 and H6)

The system refuses welding on a pressure part after the unit's final PWHT (H5), or after hydro (H6). The floor sees a red card. Only QC raises the NCR.

1. Tap **Scan**, type the unit serial, open the unit. You can read every department's steps.
2. Find the refused weld row. Tap **Raise NCR (H5)** or **Raise NCR (H6)**. If one is already open the button reads **NCR open**.
3. Open **Queue**, then **NCRs**. For this NCR you can choose only **Repair**. The unit's PWHT and hydro must then be repeated (H5), or hydro alone (H6).
4. Or the **Production Head** accepts it per a code clause (Concession). The row then reads **Awaiting QC ratification**. Tap **Ratify**. The NCR closes. You may ratify only if you are not the person who accepted it.

## Ordinary NCRs

On the **NCRs** tab, status **Needs disposition**: tap **Disposition...**, choose **Rework**, **Repair**, **Use as is**, **Concession** or **Scrap**, add notes and a rework due date. After rework, the supervisor redoes the step, then you **Accept** it and the NCR closes. You cannot verify the redone step before the disposition is saved.

## Also yours

- **Weld map**: on the job's Materials tab, open **Weld map**, check joints against the drawing, tap **Confirm N joints**. Engineering can also confirm.
- **PMI** (material check): on **Record MTC...** only QC can choose PMI accept or reject.
- **Scan** works on a phone for any unit.
